Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

The short version: there are no accounts and no ads, and we don't sell data. Most of what you enter stays on your own phone. Your location is used to show what's nearby and is never stored on our servers.

This policy explains how NOMAD Van Life OS ("NOMAD", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app on the web, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android or Windows. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following stays in your phone's or computer's storage. It is not sent to us:

  • your name, van details and chosen buddy
  • power, water, fuel, service and budget logs
  • saved spots, planned trips, free-night history, journal and badges
  • your medical card and emergency contact
  • roadside membership, insurer, policy and rego details you choose to save

Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes this information. You can also wipe it any time in Me → Delete my data.

2. Location

With your permission, NOMAD uses your device's location to find nearby camps, toilets, fuel, services and weather, to plan routes and for turn-by-turn navigation. Your location is processed on your device. To fetch data, approximate or exact coordinates are sent to the services below. We don't store your location history on our servers.

3. Services that receive limited data

  • Weather (MET Norway, usually through NOMAD’s own server): coordinates rounded to about 1 km, to get your forecast. Tide times are worked out on your phone, so no location is sent for tides.
  • Maps (OpenFreeMap, Esri satellite imagery): map tiles for the area you're viewing.
  • Routing and search (Valhalla / OSRM / Photon, built on OpenStreetMap): start and end points of a route, or text you search for.
  • Hosting (GitHub Pages): standard web server logs such as IP address and device type.
  • Community features (Google Firebase): see section 4.
  • Payments (Stripe, Apple, Google): see section 5.
  • Spotify (optional): if you connect it, a login token is stored on your device and used to show and control what's playing.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

4. Community features (Crew)

When you create a NOMAD ID, add a place or meetup, rate a toilet, report a fuel price, post a job or report something, the app creates an anonymous ID (no email or password). Your @username, avatar and anything you post are stored in Google Firebase and are visible to other NOMAD users, including the location of any place you add. Please don't post personal details. You can delete your posts with Me → Delete my data.

Backups. If you create a NOMAD ID, your app data is backed up to Google Firebase automatically. It is encrypted on your phone with your recovery key (AES-256) before it is uploaded, so we cannot read it. Without your recovery key the backup cannot be restored by anyone, including us.

5. Payments

NOMAD Pro is sold through Stripe. Stripe processes your payment. We never see or store your card details. We receive a record of the purchase (amount, date, and an email if you provided one) so we can deliver and support your subscription.

6. What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking
  • We don't sell or rent personal information
  • No location history on our servers

7. Children

NOMAD isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

8. Your choices and rights

You can turn off location in your device settings, delete your data in the app, or email us to access, correct or delete information we hold. If you're unhappy with our response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

9. Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
